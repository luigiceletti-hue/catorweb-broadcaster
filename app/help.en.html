Getting started

Catorweb Broadcaster is radio automation software: it plays music from your PC and broadcasts it live to your streaming server. You use it in your browser; the black window that opens alongside is the program itself.

  1. Open Catorweb Broadcaster (Desktop icon or Start menu).
  2. On the first start fill in the Settings: language, radio name, streaming server details, folders, microphone. Use 🔌 Test connection to check the server details.
  3. Save: the Dashboard opens. No login is needed: the app only answers on this PC.
  4. In Library → ⬆ Import import your music folder.
  5. Prepare a Queue or a Clockwheel, try it with 🎧 Rehearse, then go on air.
Closing the black window closes the program and the broadcast. Keep it open (minimised is fine) while you broadcast.

No server details yet?

No problem: in Settings leave the server address empty and save. You can use everything (library, clockwheels, scheduler, studio, microphone, recording) in 🎧 Rehearse, with the radio playing only on this PC. "Go on air" reminds you that the server is missing. When your provider sends you the details, enter them in Settings and check them with 🔌 Test connection.

Going on air

  1. Press ⚪ OFF AIR — Go on air and confirm.
  2. The button turns into 🟠 Connecting to server… and then 🔴 ON AIR.
  3. The broadcast starts from the first track in the queue; if the queue is empty the clockwheel picks.

To stop: 🔴 ON AIR — End broadcast. With ⏹ after this track the broadcast ends when the track on air finishes (and the microphone is off); you can cancel until then.

If the button stays on 🟠 Not connected the PC cannot reach the server: a message tells you why (wrong address or port, password rejected…). Check the server Settings.

While Catorweb Broadcaster is open Windows does not go to sleep. Closing a laptop lid still puts it to sleep though.

🎧 Rehearse — listen without going on air

🎧 Rehearse runs the radio exactly as if you were on air (queue, clockwheel, jingles, microphone, manual mode) but only on this PC: nothing reaches the server. Listen with 🎧 Listen.

  • While rehearsing the green button reads 🎧 REHEARSING (this PC only) — ⏹ Stop; the big button still reads OFF AIR — Go on air.
  • Pressing Go on air while rehearsing connects the server without stopping the music: the track you are listening to goes on air as it is.
  • Automatic recording does not start while rehearsing (it starts when you go on air); ⏺ REC by hand works.
  • If you change the microphone or the server in Settings while rehearsing, Rehearse restarts by itself with the new values.

🎙 Microphone

Works on air and in Rehearse. You pick it in Settings → Microphone.

  • 🎙 MIC: click = on/off (red = on).
  • Hold the Space bar: you talk as long as you hold it (not while typing in a text field).
  • Slider next to it: on-air voice volume, from −6 to +18 dB.

🤖 Auto or 🎚 Studio: when the music ducks

ModeWhat happens
🤖 Auto (default)The music ducks only while you actually speak and comes back about 1 second after your last word. You can leave the microphone on.
🎚 StudioUsed with the Space bar: hold it while you talk, the music drops straight away and comes back as soon as you let go. Like a mixer fader, you decide when. (If you switch the microphone on with 🎙 MIC instead, the music stays down until you switch it off.)
Don't confuse 🤖 Auto (microphone) with ▶ Automatic (how tracks start): they are two different things.

The voice goes through a processor (low cut, compressor, de-esser, limiter). In Settings → Microphone you set the voice pitch (−2 to +2 semitones, default 0 = natural) and the voice body (bass, default +3.5 dB). To hear the music in your headphones without your own voice use 🎧 Listen → Music only.

⏺ Recording

  • ⏺ REC: click = start (red, with the time), click = stop. Works on air and in Rehearse.
  • auto: records every broadcast by itself, from "Go on air" to "End broadcast".
  • 📂 opens the recordings folder.

Two MP3 files are created: RadioName_YYYY-MM-DD_hh-mm-ss.mp3 (exactly what goes on air, music + voice, 192 kbit/s, about 85 MB per hour) and …_voce.mp3 with the voice only, handy for editing.

On the Dashboard the Space and archive panel shows how many hours of recording still fit on the disk (about 144 MB per hour, voice file included), and how much the recordings and the library take up. Under 24 hours it turns orange, under 3 red; if you are recording a warning also appears at the top.

Library

Categories on the left (virtual folders: a track can be in several categories), tracks on the right.

  • Search and sort (artist, title, genre, duration, year, date added, random, manual order by dragging).
  • Select tracks with the checkboxes or ✓ All, then:
    • ➕ To queue adds them to the end of the queue, ↺ Replace queue clears the queue and puts the selected ones in it;
    • ↗ Move copies or moves them to another category;
    • ↩ Remove takes them out of the category (they stay in the library and on disk);
    • 🗑 Delete from database removes them from the library and every category, the queue and the jingle buttons (the files stay on disk and can be re-imported);
    • 💾 Save M3U saves a playlist.
  • + Cat creates a category. Hovering over a category shows +sub (subcategory), ✎ (rename) and ✕ (delete: choose whether to delete only the category or also all its tracks from the database, even if they are in other categories). Dragging tracks onto a category copies them there.
  • ⚙ Create from folders creates categories from your music folders.
  • 📊 Stats: category statistics (decades, genres, years).
  • Click a track's year to edit it (also for the whole album).

Tags and embargo

Click a track title: the Tag Editor opens with a preview. You can change title, artist, album, year, genre, type (song, jingle, intro, voice track…), apply the genre to the whole album and trim the silence at the start and end (the original is kept as a backup).

Embargo

A date in the Comment field (e.g. 24/10, 24/10/2026, or Italian 24 ottobre; no year = current year) puts the track under embargo up to and including that day:

  • the library shows 🔒 with the date;
  • the clockwheel never picks it and it cannot enter the queue, running order, playlists or scheduler;
  • it cannot be moved or copied to other categories and is left out of M3U/MP3 exports.

From the next day it is free automatically.

Queue

Queued tracks play before the clockwheel, in order. When the queue is empty the radio carries on with the active clockwheel. The Queue tab shows Liquidsoap's queue; 🗑 Clear empties it.

Saved playlists are added to the queue from the Dashboard (➕ Add or 🔁 Replace).

Clockwheel

A clockwheel is a "wheel" of slots: each slot draws tracks from a category with a rule. When the queue is empty the radio picks tracks from the active clockwheel.

  • Slot mode: random with rules, track not played for the longest time, most recently played track, artist not played for the longest time…; tracks per slot.
  • ⚙ Repeat rules: minutes between two tracks by the same artist or from the same album, maximum of the same artist per hour, strict separation (don't repeat within the last N tracks).
  • Stop mode (for generated playlists): Loop (finish the round), Exact (exact duration), Over (first overrun).
  • ⚡ Activate forces the clockwheel (it takes priority over the scheduler); ✖ Release removes the forcing.
  • 👁 Preview and 📀 Generate playlist for a chosen duration.

Scheduler

Timed events, in the PC's local time. Each event has a start time, a duration, a repeat (every day, specific days, once) and one or more actions:

  • use a clockwheel for that time slot (your schedule);
  • add to or replace the queue with a playlist, a category or a track;
  • skip the track, clear the queue, move tracks between categories, empty a category;
  • change the default clockwheel, back up the database.

Show timeline shows the week with a red "now" line and the block on air. ▶ Run now tries an event straight away.

A forced clockwheel (⚡ Activate) overrides the schedule: a banner at the top reminds you. Use ✖ Release to go back to the scheduler.

🎛 Studio

The tab for presenting. At the top right there is a big countdown to the end of the track, with a bar (orange in the last 30 s, flashing red in the last 10 s) and the NEXT strip.

▶ Automatic / ✋ Manual

  • Automatic: queue + clockwheel, everything starts by itself.
  • Manual: the switch happens at the end of the current track; from then on nothing plays until you press ▶ START. START while a track is playing = move straight to the next one.
  • The running order is the queue: quick search, + Running order, ✕ to remove a track.
  • Silence warning: if you don't talk and don't start anything, there is silence on air.
  • "End broadcast" always switches back to Automatic for next time.

Jingles

8 buttons in the Studio tab. Assign them from search (🎵), one click plays them, ⏹ Stop jingle stops them. They work in Automatic too. Each button has its own mode (click the label at the bottom to change it):

≋ OVERThe jingle plays over the music, which ducks. For short effects.
⏏ BREAKThe music fades out (about 1 s), the track ends and the jingle plays alone. Then in Automatic the next track starts, in Manual silence until START.

No fades: a track placed in a category whose name contains jingle (or sweeper, liner, station id, imaging) becomes a jingle and goes on air with no fade and no crossfade, from the queue, the clockwheel and the buttons. The same for categories named intro, promo, news, spot, voice track.

Sample jingles: on first start the Library already has a Jingle category with 3 sample jingles, so you can see how they work right away (in a clockwheel or on the buttons). When you have your own, remove them from the category and import yours.

Track card and intro with Claude

Under the NEXT strip you get a card for the next track: photo, biography (Wikipedia), country and years, album and year (MusicBrainz, iTunes), how many times you have played it and when. It needs internet; cards are cached for 30 days, ↻ search again refreshes them. The sources are open: check before saying it on air.

✍ Suggest intro (optional)

If Claude Code is installed on the PC and signed in to your account, Claude writes 2-3 sentences to read on the microphone using only the facts on the card. Choose Opus (writes better) or Sonnet (uses less). The text appears in the 🎙 TO READ box, which stays until you close it. Facts added by Claude are listed separately: check them. Turn it off in Settings → Extra.

Statistics and Log

  • Statistics: library overview, top tracks (exportable to M3U, also as a countdown), top artists and genres, recently played, never played.
  • Log: Activity (what happened in the app) and Liquidsoap (the audio engine). 📋 Copy to send them to someone helping you.

Maintenance

  • Short / corrupted tracks, Suspicious silences at the start or end of files.
  • Files missing from disk (in the database but no longer on disk) and Orphan files (on disk but not in the database).
  • Duplicates (same artist and title).
  • Empty categories and Missing tags, with automatic artist/title detection from the file name.
  • Backup & Restore of the database.
Actions "FROM DISK" really delete files: there is no undo.

Tools

  • M3U → MP3 Export: copies the tracks of a saved playlist (or of the queue) as MP3 files into a folder (by default the export folder from Settings). Tracks under embargo are not copied.
  • Online Tools: playlist tools on catorweb.net, they open in the browser.

Troubleshooting

It won't go on air / stays "Not connected"

Settings → 🔌 Test connection. The message tells you why: wrong address or port, password or mount rejected, server down. Check the Log tab too.

The music stays low with the microphone on

You are in 🎚 Studio and switched the microphone on with 🎙 MIC: in Studio you talk by holding the space bar. If you prefer to leave the microphone on, click the button to switch to 🤖 Auto: the music drops only while you speak.

The microphone can't be heard

Check in Settings that the right microphone is selected. It only works on air or in Rehearse. In the Log, with the microphone on, the voice level is written every 2 seconds.

"SILENCE DETECTED"

No audio has gone on air for more than 20 seconds. Wait 20-30 seconds (automatic restart); if it stays, end and restart the broadcast. In Manual, silence between tracks is normal.

"Catorweb Broadcaster is already open"

The program is already running: use the window that is already open, or close it and start the icon again.

The track card is empty

It needs internet; without an artist in the database there is no online lookup (it could easily find the wrong one).

The program uses ports 8767, 8001 and 1235 on this PC only: no router changes are needed.
